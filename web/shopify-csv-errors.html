A comma inside a title is not a new column

linen-shirt,"Linen shirt, relaxed",DEMO-SHIRT-S,29.00,Size,Small

A CSV reader treats the quoted comma as part of the title. If a title includes a literal quote, CSV escapes it by doubling it inside the quoted field. A quoted field can also contain a line break, so a record is not always a single physical line. The checker reports the record’s starting line.

COLUMN_COUNT means a parsed record has a different number of fields from the header. Inspect quoting and the export delimiter first. Do not repair it by deleting every comma: that can corrupt titles and descriptions.

Repeated handles and repeated variants are different

Multiple variants normally share a product handle. Compare that handle together with all option values. REPEATED_VARIANT asks you to inspect a repeated combination; it does not automatically remove or merge anything. Image-only continuation rows can repeat a handle without representing another variant.

REPEATED_SKU is a separate warning. Reused SKUs can be intentional in some workflows. Confirm the intended product and variant identities before changing them.

Price formatting cannot establish the right price

PRICE_FORMAT flags strings that do not match this checker’s nonnegative decimal format. Compare the intended amount and currency against your source and Shopify’s current specification. Replacing a comma or deleting a currency symbol without checking can turn a formatting change into a wrong selling price.

Keep variant dependencies when trimming an update

Shopify’s product CSV documentation explains that variant-related updates depend on option columns. Missing option information can replace existing variants with a default variant. A checker that only sees the file cannot establish what will happen to the current store.

Similarly, an included blank field and an omitted column can behave differently when overwriting products. Review the import and handoff checklist, keep an appropriate recovery plan, and test a small recoverable batch in the actual platform.

# First discovery and skeptical selection — 2026-10-18

This is opportunity research, not proof of demand. Exa returned 20 search results across four targeted searches covering platform import problems, competing utilities, catalog migration competitors and other data-source faults. Nine primary vendor/platform pages were then fetched directly. Their dated source register, reuse notes, confidence, original summaries and all ten candidate reviews are in `opportunities.json`. No customer, interview, traffic, signup, order or captured payment has been observed. Only facts were paraphrased; no vendor copy or product implementation was reused. No content-republication license is assumed, and no accounts, subscriptions or purchases were created.

Scoring follows the master prompt: urgency 25%, reachable distribution 20%, rapid fulfillment 20%, achievable margin 15%, defensibility 10%, safety 10%. Each criterion is an analyst judgment from 0 to 5; the weighted score is their weighted sum divided by five. Margin scores reflect inexpensive local computation, not verified contribution margin or conversion. The candidate-level counterarguments and assumptions remain visible in the machine-readable register.

The full selection and tie-break order are computed by the worker; the JSON is the source of truth. The winner's exploratory differentiation is a local, credential-free agency preflight with value-free row diagnostics and current/legacy heading support. This is a hypothesis, not an established moat. The prototype deliberately implements a limited subset and avoids changing original data. A price of EUR 9 is only an unvalidated hypothesis; the evaluator is free and has no payment control.

The clearest problem evidence is the [Shopify error documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/common-import-issues) and [format guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/import-export/using-csv). Strong counterevidence is [Matrixify's existing dry run](https://matrixify.app/documentation/matrixify-import-export-job-options/) and [free/paid offer](https://matrixify.app/pricing/), plus [WooCommerce's built-in importer](https://woocommerce.com/document/product-csv-importer-exporter/). Published prices show competing offers, not independently verified willingness to pay.

Next eligible validation is an original guide and synthetic example on permitted, cost-controlled infrastructure after the operator/privacy notice is ready. Seek only voluntary feedback or explicitly permitted public participation; do not send unsolicited contacts. Define the observation window, actual traffic and useful-completion metrics when a real URL exists. Evaluate whether users report an unsolved problem before building a paid tier; no positive conversion or sales projection is justified now. If permitted distribution cannot reach buyers or existing tools suffice, prefer the alternates or a fresh discovery pass over expanding this clone.

Public RSS refresh is optional and separate from candidate selection. A refresh does not update the dated vendor evidence or validate demand. Source terms/robots must be rechecked before broad automated crawling; this pilot has no arbitrary crawler, outreach tool or publishing permission.

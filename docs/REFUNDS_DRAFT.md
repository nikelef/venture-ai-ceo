# Refunds and withdrawal — draft requiring legal review

No purchases are being accepted. Do not publish this as an operative refund policy.

Before enabling sales, determine the applicable consumer, tax and digital-content rules through qualified review and the provider's secure business onboarding. Complete seller/support identity, eligibility, delivery failure remedies, statutory rights, withdrawal process, any required digital-content consents, response times, refund method and records retention. Never silently waive rights or promise a rule that has not been reviewed.

Refund transactions must use the licensed provider's dashboard or a separately approved refund integration. This pilot cannot initiate a refund, outgoing transfer or payout change. Reconciliation tests only compute synthetic refunds; they never move funds or notify a customer.

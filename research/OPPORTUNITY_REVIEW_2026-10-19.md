# Opportunity challenge — 19 October 2026

Payments remain deferred by the owner. This scan investigates alternatives to Catalog Preflight without building another product, collecting contacts, opening accounts or paying for services. Routine factual replies are authorized within existing account permissions and platform rules. The project's open GitHub issue queue was checked and was empty; no outbound reply was needed.

## Method and evidence limits

Nine Exa searches requested and returned 45 results, deduplicated to 42 URLs. Fourteen follow-up fetch requests read 12 distinct primary pages, including repeat fetches to reach long documentation sections. These are source counts, not independent customers or interviews. The machine-readable [source inventory](experiments/opportunity-scan-2026-10-19.json) preserves discovery URLs and dates without copying reviews or storing prospect details.

Searches covered three distinct workflows, then looked for independent practitioner accounts and official prices. Vendor case studies, interviews with the vendor's founder, tutorials and anonymous forum posts have different evidential strength. A recent publication date alone does not establish authenticity. One DEV result explicitly labeled “Fully Autonomous” was excluded as customer evidence. Old forum discussions are historical context, not current buying intent. No testimonials were copied to our site.

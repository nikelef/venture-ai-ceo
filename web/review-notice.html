Using the checker

CSV files, filenames, headings, cell values and full diagnostic reports are processed only in your browser. They are not sent to our collector. Downloaded JSON reports omit source headings and cell values. Clear the file to release its contents from the page. Browser print and saved files remain under your control.

Separate, optional choices

You may use the checker without agreeing to measurement or registering. Measurement requires an affirmative click after you choose a role. It sends a random session token, broad source category, role, consent version and fixed event names. It uses page memory, without cookies, local storage, session storage, advertising IDs or fingerprinting. Reloading starts without consent. Our collector stores a hash of the token and coarse event flags; it does not store IP addresses, user agents, exact referring URLs, catalog contents or file sizes.

Voluntary registration needs a separate, unchecked consent box. It saves your work email, role, platform, planned import timing, selected problem, optional pilot request and your chosen yes/no/unsure answer at the proposed €9 price. We store the contact encrypted and a keyed hash for duplicate detection. We do not ask for your name, store credentials, payment credentials, catalog uploads or private business documents. Registration permits a response about this particular pilot or your feedback; it does not enroll you in a newsletter or unrelated marketing.

Retention, processors and access

Optional records have a 30-day retention target, with automated daily deletion and deletion on activity. If the collector or scheduler is unavailable, removal may be delayed until service resumes; collection stops when capacity or provider quotas are exhausted. De-identified aggregate experiment reports may be retained longer. Hosting providers may keep their own operational logs and limited restore copies under their policies.

Render hosts the public pages. Neon hosts the separate collector and its Frankfurt database. These providers receive ordinary connection metadata, including IP addresses, when you use their services. Owner-authorized project administrators can access the research database. Contact decryption requires a server secret. Personal registrations are not copied into GitHub source, research artifacts or public reports. The collector rejects arbitrary extra fields.

Withdraw or correct

Use “Stop & delete this session” on the open page to withdraw measurement and request deletion of that session. If confirmation fails, retry while the page remains open. Closing the page stops future measurement; its stored flags expire under the retention policy. A registration has a separate private receipt: save it, then use “Withdraw an expression of interest” to delete it. Deletion replies do not reveal whether a receipt existed. To correct an answer, delete and submit a new registration. Keep receipts private. The operator privacy contact will be supplied before activation for access requests, lost receipts, questions and complaints. You may also raise a concern with your data protection authority.

The free checker

Your CSV, filename, headings, cell values and full report stay in your browser. The collector never receives them. Downloaded JSON reports omit source headings and cell values. Clear the selected file to release it from the page; printing and downloads remain under your control. Hosting still requires ordinary network connections.

Optional measurement

Only an affirmative measurement choice starts this processing, based on your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can refuse and use the full free checker. We collect a chosen role, broad acquisition category, consent version, session creation time and fixed flags for engagement, product interaction, sample use, a confirmed real-file evaluation and report download. The server stores a hash of a random session token, not the token itself. These flags help assess use of the tool, not uniquely identify people.

No analytics cookies, advertising identifiers, local/session storage or fingerprinting are used. The token stays in page memory and expires for new activity after four hours. Reloading starts without measurement consent. The application does not store IPs, user agents, exact referring URLs, file sizes or file contents. Providers can receive connection metadata separately.

Separate voluntary registration

A separate, unchecked registration box asks for consent to process your work email and answers for this specific validation experiment and to respond about your pilot request or feedback (Article 6(1)(a)). Measurement consent is not required. Registration saves email, role, platform, planned import timing, selected problem, optional free-pilot request, source category, consent version, creation time and your chosen yes/no/unsure response to the proposed €9 total for one pack.

Email is encrypted with authenticated AES-256 encryption. A keyed hash detects duplicate addresses. A separate hashed receipt supports deletion. We do not request names, store credentials, payment details, uploads or business documents. Registration is for adults, is non-binding, creates no purchase and does not verify email ownership or confirm a pilot place. No answer about willingness to pay is preselected. There is no newsletter, resale or unrelated marketing.

Essential processing and lawful bases

Serving pages, protecting the service and handling support requests rely on legitimate interests in operating a safe, usable service (Article 6(1)(f)); optional experiment measurement does not use this basis. Handling applicable statutory privacy requests relies on legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Email correspondence can contain your address and message; send only information needed for your request. Do not email catalogs or credentials. We make no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Retention and deletion

Experiment records expire after 30 days. Purging runs before each new consented session and through the aggregate monitor. During an outage deletion can be delayed until service resumes; a 30-day expiry is not a guarantee about the timing of provider backups. Records can be deleted sooner using the controls below. Capacity or provider quota exhaustion stops new collection. Anonymous totals in private experiment reports may remain longer; small counts are not published with identifying details.

Support and rights correspondence is retained as needed to handle and document the request, meet applicable legal obligations or resolve a dispute; ask the privacy contact about the period for your request. Provider security logs and restore copies follow provider policies. This project's database has a six-hour restore-history setting. A deletion can remain in a limited restore copy until it expires; restoring data requires reapplying outstanding deletion requests before collection resumes.

Hosting, recipients and transfers

  • Render serves the public pages through its hosting/CDN. It receives ordinary request metadata. No CSV is uploaded to Render.
  • Neon / Databricks runs the collector and database in AWS Frankfurt, Germany. It receives consented API submissions and connection metadata. Application contact encryption does not prevent the hosting runtime processing an email when it is submitted. See Neon's current terms and subprocessors and the incorporated Databricks DPA.
  • GitHub hosts private source and runs the aggregate-only monitor. Contacts, receipts, session records and catalog data are excluded from its artifacts. Aggregate artifacts expire after seven days.
  • If you email us, Proton processes that correspondence under its Swiss service and DPA. Ordinary external email is not guaranteed end-to-end encrypted.

Authorized CeoAgentAI project administrators and the service providers can access data within their respective roles. Contact decryption needs a separate server secret. An EU database region does not make all provider operations EU-only: support, security and subprocessors can involve other countries, including the United States; Proton is based in Switzerland. The linked provider terms describe their applicable transfer safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where required. Request information or a copy of relevant safeguards at the privacy email. We do not assert that every transfer or provider log-retention practice has been independently audited.

Withdraw, access or correct

Use “Stop & delete this session” on the open page to stop measurement and delete that session. If confirmation fails, retry while the page and token remain available. Closing or reloading stops future events but does not itself delete stored flags. Registration is separate: save the private receipt and use “Withdraw an expression of interest” to delete that registration. These controls continue to accept deletion while enrollment is paused. Deletion replies do not reveal whether a receipt existed. Keep receipts private.

For access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, objection to legitimate-interest processing, lost receipts or any question, email ceoagentai@proton.me. We may need proportionate information to match a request; do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents unsolicited. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. You can delete and submit corrected registration answers. We will address rights requests within the applicable time limit, normally one month.

You may complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, or the competent authority where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. See that authority's submission procedure.

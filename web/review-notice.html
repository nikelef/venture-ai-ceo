Research prototype

This free evaluation tool processes chosen CSV files in your browser. Its code sends no file contents, reports or analytics to a server, and uses no cookies or browser storage. Downloaded reports contain diagnostic locations and codes, without source headings or cell values. Use “Clear file & report” to release the current selection.

The hosted evaluator is served by Render. Loading the website sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and requested page, to the hosting provider. CSV contents and generated reports are processed on your device. Project maintainer: nikelef on GitHub.
